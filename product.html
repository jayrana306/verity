How it works

Record once. Get the documents your profession needs.

Verity is a native macOS and iOS app for client-facing professionals. With everyone’s consent, it records the conversation and turns it into the exact documents your profession requires — every line traceable to what was actually said.

Try the prototypeJoin the pilot

Six stages, one review step

The product is the review step, not the transcript. Every stage exists to make a draft safe to sign.

  1. CaptureIn person, from a call, by phone or upload — after consent.
  2. TranscribeStreaming speech-to-text with speaker separation.
  3. ExtractFacts pulled out with timestamps.
  4. ValidateEvery claim checked against the transcript.
  5. RenderVersioned, profession-specific templates.
  6. ApproveThe professional reviews, signs and delivers.

Five explicit claim states

Every statement in a draft carries one of five states.

Source-linkedVerifiedNeeds reviewExplicitly deniedNot documented

Verity never fills a gap. If an exam wasn’t performed or a jurisdiction wasn’t named, the document says so — and signing is blocked until the professional resolves it.

Built on real professional standards

Medical

SOAP note & patient summary

A clinical note, a patient-facing summary, structured data for the record system, and the follow-ups you promised.

Therapy

DAP / BIRP progress notes

Follows behavioral-health documentation guidance: the golden thread to treatment goals, mental-status findings, risk and CPT time.

Legal

Intake memo & chronology

Law-office memorandum format — Question Presented, Brief Answer, Facts, Discussion, Conclusion — checked live against the firm’s intake checklist.

Consulting

Meeting notes & actions

Decisions, owners and follow-ups, delivered as tasks to the tools your team already uses.

What makes Verity different

01

One recording, many documents

A single consultation produces every document your profession needs — not just a transcript.

02

Evidence for every line

Each statement links to the moment in the transcript it came from.

03

Templates behave like software

Create a template, test it on a sample session, review a validation report and publish a version. Published changes never alter documents that were already signed.

04

Approval, audit and delivery

Signed documents are read-only; changes go in a dated addendum. Every consent, edit, verification, signature and delivery is logged.

05

Apple-native

Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and iOS 26. Start recording on iPhone, finish reviewing on the Mac with Handoff.

06

No bot in your call

Virtual calls are captured on the Mac — nothing joins the meeting on your behalf.

What Verity deliberately doesn’t do

No clinical decision support, diagnoses or legal conclusions. Verity documents what was said; the professional decides.
