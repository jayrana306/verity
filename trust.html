Trust & security

Built to be signed, not just generated.

Professionals sign records, so the defensible product is the one that makes a draft safe to sign. These are the commitments Verity is designed around.

Our commitments

All-party consent by default

Recording starts only after every participant is named and consent is verified and timestamped.

Audio you control

Raw audio is deleted after transcription by default, and a no-audio mode is available.

No training on your data

Customer recordings, transcripts and documents are never used to train models.

Configurable retention

Each organization sets its own retention policy for transcripts and documents.

Complete audit trail

Consent, capture, AI draft, edits, verifications, addenda, approval and delivery — all logged and exportable.

Signed means signed

Approved documents are read-only. Late entries go in a dated, labelled addendum.

How the platform is built

  • Tenant isolation. PostgreSQL with row-level isolation between organizations.
  • Encrypted storage. Recordings and documents live in encrypted object storage.
  • Access control. MFA for everyone; SSO on the Team plan and above.
  • Psychotherapy notes kept separate from progress notes, with private session prep.
  • Privilege safeguards for legal work product, and BAAs for healthcare customers.
  • Vendor review. Speech and model vendors pass security and contractual review before any regulated data is processed.
